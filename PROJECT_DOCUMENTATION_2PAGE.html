WhiteCode Meet Automation

End-to-End Flow & Key Specification: Automated Google Meet Discussion Capture, Multilingual AI Processing & Tasksheet Logging

1 Executive Overview & Problem Solved

Eliminates manual employee timesheet entry by tracking actual Google Meet participation, transcribing English, Marathi (मराठी), and "Mar-glish" code-switching verbatim with Google Gemini 3.6 Flash, classifying projects, and pushing validated hours directly into individual corporate tasksheets and the WhiteCode HR REST API.

3 Complete End-to-End System Flow

1 Meeting Discovery

Google Calendar webhook triggers on scheduled events, or Chrome Extension instantly detects impromptu calls on meet.google.com.

2 Dual Audio & Captions

Chrome Extension records 128kbps Opus audio & observes DOM captions, stripping Google Meet system toasts ("mic muted", "user left").

3 Multilingual Gemini STT

Audio is uploaded to Gemini Files API. Gemini 3.6 Flash transcribes verbatim Marathi Devanagari script and English technical keywords.

4 Topic Extraction & AI

Gemini analyzes transcript vs. project catalog; outputs Project ID, 2–5 bullet topics discussed, and formal corporate task sentences.

5 Duration Interval Math

Mathematical interval union merges overlapping reconnects/multi-device joins so employees are never overbilled or double-counted.

6 Individual Tasksheet Sync

Creates entry in each attending employee's tasksheet with formatted effort ("1 hr 30 mins") & pushes to WhiteCode HR API.

Overlapping Interval Duration Formula: Ensures multi-device attendance (phone + laptop) or network disconnects are merged: $$\text{Total Duration} = \sum (\text{Merged Interval End} - \text{Merged Interval Start})$$ Waits until participant leaves call before generating tasksheet entry.
Identity Disambiguation: 3-tier matching: (1) employee_email_aliases lookup, (2) Direct email match, (3) Heuristic token scoring (+2000 for distinctive tokens, -1000 for mismatched names like Suraj Dantrao vs Suraj WhiteCode).

4 Backend Processing Architecture (src/index.ts)

Google Calendar Webhook & Watcher Webhook at /google/calendar/webhook verifies x-goog-channel-id against SQLite table calendar_watch_channels. Upon reception, it queries calendar.events.list, resolves Google Meet URLs (meet.google.com/xxx-yyyy-zzz), and stores meeting records in SQLite with status DETECTED.
Multilingual Audio Pipeline (gemini.service.ts) POST /api/meet/audio-chunk receives multipart WebM audio from extension. It uploads the buffer to Google AI Files API and invokes Gemini 3.6 Flash. Spoken Marathi is transcribed into Devanagari script, English tech terms (API, database, UI, test) remain in English, and ambient noise is filtered.
AI Discussion Extraction & Translation When finalized, Gemini classifies the meeting against available projects and translates Marathi discussion points into professional corporate English:
"Attended project meeting to discuss client information support requirements and automated data entry workflow."
Individual Tasksheet & External Push Validates that duration > 0 and participant has left the call. Creates individual entries in tasksheet_entries for each employee. If EXTERNAL_SYNC_ENABLED=true, immediately posts to WhiteCode REST API with automatic background retries for failed syncs.

6 Core REST Endpoints & Database Schema

Method Endpoint Payload / Params Action
GET /oauth2callback code, scope Persists refreshed Google tokens into token.json.
POST /api/meet/audio-chunk audio (WebM), meetingCode Transcribes audio with Gemini and auto-updates tasksheets.
POST /api/meet/finalize-discussion meetingCode, participants Extracts 2–5 discussion topics and creates tasksheet entries.
GET /tasksheets employeeId, date Visual dashboard to inspect, filter, and edit employee timesheets.
POST /api/sync/external None Triggers 2-way sync with WhiteCode's HR/Tasksheet REST API.

SQLite Schema (meetings.db): meetings (records, status, project), meeting_transcripts (verbatim chunks), meeting_participants & meeting_participant_sessions (intervals), employees & employee_email_aliases (directory), projects (catalog), tasksheet_entries (hours, topics, sync_status).

7 Quick Start in 4 Steps

1. Dependencies & Keys: Place credentials.json in root, set GEMINI_API_KEY in .env, run npm install.
2. Start Backend: Run npm run start (server starts on port 3000). Visit http://localhost:3000/ to grant Google permissions.
3. Load Extension: Open chrome://extensions, enable Developer Mode, click "Load unpacked", select chrome-extension folder.
4. Live Test: Join any Google Meet call, click extension icon, click "Start Discussion Tracking", speak, click "Finalize & Fill Tasksheet", and view updated entries on /tasksheets!
